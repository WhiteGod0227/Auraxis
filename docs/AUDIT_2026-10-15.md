## Quality and simulation policy

Fixed expiries are separate throughout; rolling uses only preceding finite spreads. The preserved policy includes finite non-tradable observations and excludes undefined spreads. It has a real effect: GOLDTEN–GOLDGUINEA February 2027's latest window includes eight non-tradable observations out of ten; its mean is −0.7246831160127633%. Restricting history to eligible observations would leave only two and make the mean unavailable at min_periods=5. That alternative was calculated for audit only, not applied. January 2027 includes one non-tradable observation; its current mean is −0.63399810157588%, versus −0.6372270618594268% under an eligible-only window. API assumptions and Methodology disclose this policy.

Interpolation retains real brackets and has no extrapolation fallback. All real component legs receive volume/OI checks. Invalid prices do not become zeros or enter spread histories. Roll-down retains previous-curve local-linear approximation and raw change where calculable, while invalid decomposition estimates stay unavailable. Residuals are not proven mispricing.

Fractional default October GOLDTEN–GOLDPETAL remains **0 completed / 1 open / 0 unresolved**, realized net **Rs 0**, marked net **Rs −991.7025170431716**, incurred costs **Rs 1000**. Optional integer mode on the same parameters has signed lots **A −6 / B +67**, marked net **Rs −235.7305**, costs **Rs 944.7305**. Different quantities explain the different result; parameters were not tuned. The reference denominator remains twice target notional, not margin or actual required cash. Outstanding outcomes remain unfinished.

No actual expiry DDR/settlement dataset or applicable verified fee schedule was provided; positions are not force-closed at manufactured prices. Open, unresolved, blocked exits, stale marks, valuation gaps and closed-trade cost exclusions remain distinct. Existing executed-event mapping for exit signal z versus execution-date z remains intact and regression-tested.
